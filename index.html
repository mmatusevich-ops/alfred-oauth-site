Private AI assistant

Alfred Hermes

A private assistant that helps its authorized owner and family organize work, research, documents, schedules, and routine operations through natural language.

Connected services

Purpose of Google access

Alfred connects to Google services only after an authorized user grants access. It may read or create documents in Google Drive and Docs, search or draft email, read calendar information, and prepare reports from Google Analytics and Google Ads.

Access is used solely to complete requests from authorized users. Advertising and analytics integrations are read-only by default. Sending email or making an external change requires an explicit user instruction and the configured approval policy.

Operating principles

Private by design

Limited access

Only explicitly authorized accounts can use the private deployment.

User-directed

Google data is accessed only to provide features requested by the user.

No data sale

Personal information and Google user data are never sold or used for advertising.
